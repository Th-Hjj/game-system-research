# Research method

Start with the game and the question, not a preferred engine or tool. Inspect relevant files, choose a focused route and trace findings to the conditions under which they affect play.

Explain the system before its details: what happens, what the player or other entities do, and how the main parts connect. Rules should refine that picture rather than form an isolated catalogue of parameters.

Keep both the overview and detailed rules grounded in evidence. Separate static findings, runtime observations and uncertainty; store technical material outside the main report.

The [Skill instructions](../skills/game-system-research/SKILL.md) define the workflow. [Scope and checks](validation.md) describe its limitations.
